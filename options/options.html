Keyboard shortcuts

Press Alt+K for ChatGPT, Alt+G for Gemini, or Alt+N to start a new temporary chat. On macOS, use the Option key instead of Alt.

Chat overlay

Applied when Gemini first opens or you start a new temporary chat (Alt+N). Existing conversations keep their model. Model availability depends on your Google account.

Sign-in settings

ChatGPT sign-in cookies are not sent to frames embedded on other websites by default. This option changes chatgpt.com cookies to SameSite=None, which helps keep the overlay signed in on other websites. This weakens protection against cross-site request forgery (CSRF). Disabling it may leave the overlay signed out on other websites. Browser cookie restrictions may still prevent sign-in.
