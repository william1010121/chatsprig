Ask here. Stay here.

ChatSprig opens temporary ChatGPT conversations inside your current web page. Ask a quick question while reading, studying, or working, then return to the page without opening a separate ChatGPT window.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Open a centered, resizable ChatGPT overlay on supported websites.
• Toggle the chat with Alt+K on Windows or Option+K on macOS.
• Start a fresh temporary chat with Alt+N / Option+N.
• Use a floating launcher and choose its corner.
• Hide the embedded ChatGPT sidebar and automatically focus the prompt.
• Copy supported mathematical expressions on ChatGPT pages as LaTeX source.
• Click the extension toolbar icon to open Settings.

YOU CHOOSE THE CONTEXT
ChatSprig does not automatically read or summarize the surrounding webpage. Type or paste the context you want to discuss into ChatGPT.

ACCOUNT AND COMPATIBILITY
ChatSprig embeds the ChatGPT website. Sign in to ChatGPT in a normal tab first if needed. OpenAI account, plan, usage, and temporary-chat retention rules apply. Chrome internal pages, the Chrome Web Store, some PDF viewers, and other restricted pages cannot display the overlay. Popups and top-level navigation from the embedded frame are blocked. Browser restrictions or changes to ChatGPT may affect loading and sign-in.

PRIVACY AND COOKIE COMPATIBILITY
There is no developer-operated analytics or conversation backend. Settings use Chrome Sync; messages are handled directly by the ChatGPT website.

Cross-site sign-in compatibility is enabled by default. It rewrites eligible ChatGPT cookies, including session cookies, to SameSite=None; Secure in your browser. This relaxes SameSite-based protection and can affect other ChatGPT tabs. Disabling this option stops future rewrites but does not restore cookies already changed. The extension also removes frame-blocking response headers from ChatGPT subframe responses to allow embedding. Third-party-cookie blocking can still prevent sign-in.
